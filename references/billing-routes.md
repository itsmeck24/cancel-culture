# Where to cancel, by who bills the student

Pick the route by the **biller** (who charges the card), not by the app's brand. An app bought through the
App Store is cancelled with Apple even if the email has the app's logo. Uninstalling an app never cancels it.

Pages below were checked on 2026-10-24. If one no longer loads or says something different, search the
biller's own help site again and use what it says now.

## Apple (App Store, Apple ID receipts from apple.com)

- Official page: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428 ("Cancel a subscription from Apple").
- On iPhone: Settings, tap your name, Subscriptions, pick the subscription, Cancel Subscription.
- On the web, Apple's page points to the subscriptions page at account.apple.com.
- Apple's page says to cancel a trial "at least 24 hours before the trial ends". The script already moves
  the deadline 24 hours earlier when the biller is Apple and no cancel-by date was stated.

## Google Play (receipts from Google Play / googleplay-noreply@google.com)

- Official page: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7018481
  ("Cancel, pause, or change a subscription on Google Play").
- Steps there: open the subscriptions page in Google Play, pick the subscription, Cancel subscription,
  follow the prompts.
- The page says uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription, and access continues for the
  period already paid.

## Billed directly by the service

1. Find the service's own help or account page: search the web for "<service> cancel subscription" and
   open only a result on the service's own domain (the domain the trial email came from, or the one the
   student signs in to).
2. Read the page and give the student the exact menu path it shows (for example Account, Billing,
   Cancel), plus the link on its own line.
3. If the page says cancelling ends access right away, say so before the student cancels.
4. If no official page can be found or read, say so, and tell the student to look under Account or
   Billing after signing in. Do not guess a URL.

## Any other biller (PayPal, a carrier, a school portal)

Find that biller's official help page the same way as above and route the student there. If it can't be
verified, say the route is unresolved.

## Links from the email itself

Trial emails often contain "manage subscription" links. Use one only if it is on the same official domain
as the sender and contains no sign-in token or one-time code. Never pass on a link from a domain that
merely looks similar. When in doubt, use the help page you found yourself.
